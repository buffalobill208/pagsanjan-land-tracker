## Market Read and Pricing Recommendation

The market remains static at week 15. No new sellers have entered the dataset and no listings have cleared since the Pila Bulilan Norte departure in week 14 (probable exit at PHP 2,000/sqm — still the only clearing-price signal in the dataset).

The Sisilmin/Caliraya parcel's 21% price cut (from PHP 23.5M to PHP 18.5M, disclosed this week in a new agent listing) is the only market movement observed. The property remains unsold at PHP 18,500,000 (PHP 2,204/sqm), and the original listing at PHP 23,503,200 is still live. This suggests the seller is shopping for agents willing to list at a lower price, rather than reducing the ask on the existing listing. The continued simultaneous presence of both prices in the market is a sign of seller disagreement or listing fragmentation, not a confirmed price drop.

**For a raw 0.5–1 ha parcel in Pagsanjan proper:**

- Recommended asking range: PHP 1,400–1,800/sqm (supported by the Pagsanjan-core data; above the PHP 1,300/sqm core median to allow negotiation room)
- Fastest-sale price: PHP 900–1,000/sqm (below core median; likely to transact faster given the only confirmed exit in the dataset was at PHP 2,000/sqm in a ring municipality, not core Pagsanjan)

The fifteen-week persistence of every listed parcel without any price reduction (except the Caliraya lake property via a new agent posting) suggests asking prices across the board are above where buyers are willing to transact. The market appears thin and slow-moving. Sellers seeking a quick exit should price significantly below the dataset median of PHP 1,750/sqm.
