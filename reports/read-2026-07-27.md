# Pagsanjan Farmland Market Read — 27 July 2026

**Baseline run.** No prior snapshot existed, so there is no week-over-week diff this
week. Everything below is a first reading; from next week the tracker will report new,
changed, gone, and aging listings against this file.

**Coverage:** 17 rows — 16 VERIFIED (Dot Property individual posts, each opened and
checked) and 1 INDICATIVE (OnePropertee search snippet). 9 rows are Pagsanjan-proper,
8 are in the nearby ring (Santa Cruz, Pila, Magdalena, Cavinti, Liliw).

## The range

Verified asking prices run **₱269/sqm to ₱3,000/sqm**, with a **median of ₱1,200/sqm**
across the whole comparable set. Splitting core from ring is the more useful cut:

- **Pagsanjan proper (8 verified):** ₱500 – ₱3,000/sqm, **median ₱975/sqm**
- **Nearby ring (8 verified):** ₱269 – ₱3,000/sqm, **median ₱1,600/sqm**

The intuition that Pagsanjan carries a premium over the surrounding towns does **not**
hold in this week's data — it is the other way round. Pagsanjan's median sits roughly
40% *below* the ring median. That is a composition effect, not a discount: the ring
sample is dominated by road-frontage and lake-view parcels in Santa Cruz, Cavinti and
Liliw priced at ₱2,000–₱3,000/sqm, while Pagsanjan's sample includes genuinely
back-lot agricultural parcels (Buboy at ₱500, Anibong at ₱547) that pull its median
down. The cheap floor is not in Cavinti or Lumban as one might expect — it is in the
flat rice/agricultural land west of the river: **Mojon, Pila at ₱269/sqm** and
**Calios, Santa Cruz at ₱450/sqm**, both large (3–4 ha) parcels.

## Where the bands sit

Reading the distribution rather than the extremes, the market sorts into three fairly
clean bands:

- **₱269 – ₱700/sqm — bulk agricultural.** Large parcels (1.3–3.8 ha), interior or
  minimal frontage, sold as farmland rather than as development land. Buboy, Anibong,
  Barangay I, Calios, Mojon.
- **₱850 – ₱1,500/sqm — the working middle.** Titled, flat, road-accessible parcels
  that read as "ready for a hobby farm or a small subdivision." Lambac, Pinagsanjan,
  Sabang, Bucal, Pag-Asa. This is the densest part of the set and it is where
  Pagsanjan-proper inventory clusters.
- **₱2,000 – ₱3,000/sqm — amenity and frontage.** Fruit-bearing, river or lake
  frontage, highway corner lots, or small parcels where the buyer is paying for
  location rather than hectares. Maulawin, Bubukal, Ibabang San Roque, Sumucab,
  Sampaloc, Cigaras.

Notably, the two Pagsanjan parcels that actually sit in the owner's size range —
**Maulawin (4,767 sqm) at ₱2,000/sqm** — price at the top band, not the middle. Small
parcels command a meaningful per-sqm premium here: the three sub-1-ha comparables in
the whole set (Maulawin 0.48 ha, Pag-Asa 0.47 ha, Sumucab 0.84 ha) ask ₱2,000, ₱1,200
and ₱2,204/sqm respectively, against a ₱975 Pagsanjan median dragged down by 5 ha
blocks. **Per-sqm price and parcel size are inversely related in this data, and that
is the single most decision-relevant pattern in it.**

## Aging vs cleared inventory

**This cannot be assessed yet, and it would be dishonest to imply otherwise.** Neither
Dot Property nor Lamudi publishes listing dates, OnePropertee's "updated X ago" stamp
did not appear on any row that survived verification, and there is no prior snapshot to
diff against. No days-on-market figure has been invented. The "what ₱/sqm band sells
fastest" question is genuinely unanswerable from a single week's data — it requires the
GONE-listing signal, which arrives from next week's run onward. Several of these
parcels (the 5 ha Lambac and Pinagsanjan blocks in particular) look like long-standing
inventory given how they are marketed, but that is an impression, not a measurement.

## Pricing recommendation

**For a raw 0.5–1 ha parcel in Pagsanjan proper, ask ₱1,600–₱1,800/sqm — roughly
₱8.0M–₱9.0M on 5,000 sqm, or ₱16M–₱18M on a full hectare — and expect to transact
near ₱1,400/sqm.**

The reasoning: the Pagsanjan median of ₱975/sqm is set by 5 ha blocks and is the wrong
anchor for a small parcel. The right anchor is the sub-1-ha comparable set, which asks
₱1,200–₱2,204/sqm. Maulawin at ₱2,000/sqm is the closest true comparable — same
municipality, same size class, fruit-bearing, road frontage — and it is still on the
market, which argues against matching it. Pricing at ₱1,600–₱1,800 undercuts the
nearest Pagsanjan comparable by 10–20% while staying well clear of the ₱850–₱1,100
bulk-block pricing that would leave money on the table.

For **quickest sale specifically**, price at **₱1,400/sqm** (₱7.0M on 5,000 sqm). That
sits just above the ₱1,200 whole-market median, below every small-parcel comparable in
the set, and inside the ₱850–₱1,500 working-middle band where the most inventory
competes — visible as a value listing without signalling distress. If the parcel has
river access or standing fruit-bearing trees, hold the ₱1,800 end; if it is interior
with no frontage, start at ₱1,400 and treat ₱1,100 as the floor.

Revisit this after two or three weekly runs, when GONE listings start to show which
bands actually clear rather than which bands merely list.

## Honesty notes

- OnePropertee was the weak source this week. Its Pagsanjan snippets repeatedly gave
  contradictory figures for what appears to be the same 2,000 sqm listing (₱600,000,
  ₱2.30M, and ₱6.38M, with three different ₱/sqm claims). All were dropped rather than
  guessed at. Only one OnePropertee row survived reconciliation.
- Lamudi was used for context only, as required. It independently corroborated four
  Dot Property rows (51,328 / 29,434 / 4,767 / 10,454 sqm), which is a useful
  cross-check on the verified set. It surfaced one further Pagsanjan parcel — Dingin,
  13,926 sqm at ₱1,250/sqm — with no stated total price; it is not reconcilable and so
  is not a row.
- Dot Property's nearby-town pages are unreliable on location tagging: the Lumban and
  Majayjay land pages were largely filled with Nuvali, Sta Rosa, Carmona and Silang
  listings that are far outside scope. Every nearby row here was opened individually
  and its municipality confirmed from the post body, not the page tag. **Lumban
  contributed no usable rows and Pakil returned zero results.**
- Rows with structures were excluded even where the land pricing was attractive, and
  three parcels at 9–20 ha were excluded as too large to compare.
